Yes, when done correctly. We always take a full NANDroid backup before flashing any ROM. Custom ROMs from trusted sources (LineageOS, PixelOS etc.) are safe, actively maintained, and often more secure than stock OEM ROMs due to faster security patch delivery.

Most ROM installations require a full wipe (factory reset). We recommend backing up your WhatsApp, photos, contacts and apps beforehand. We can guide you through the backup process before we begin. Rooting alone does NOT erase your data.

Yes! With Magisk's DenyList feature and proper configuration, most banking apps including MTN Mobile Money and Airtel Money work fine on rooted devices. We set this up as part of our rooting service at no extra cost.

Basic services (rooting, recovery install) take 30–60 minutes. Custom ROM flashing takes 1–2 hours. Complex jobs like IMEI repair or kernel tuning may take up to a day. We'll give you a clear timeline before starting.

Yes! Soft bricks (device won't boot, stuck on logo) can almost always be fixed via recovery or EDL/Fastboot mode. Hard bricks (device completely dead) may require hardware tools — contact us and we'll assess. Don't attempt to fix a brick yourself; you can make it worse.

Some software tasks can be guided remotely via WhatsApp video call or step-by-step instructions. However, most modding work (especially hardware-related) requires physical device access at our Namanve location.

We primarily support Qualcomm Snapdragon (600–8xx series) and MediaTek Helio (G and P series) for overclocking. Samsung Exynos support depends on the specific model. Ask us about your chipset on WhatsApp.
