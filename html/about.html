// my journey

The Story

2021

The Spark

Got my first Xiaomi device. Frustrated by MIUI bloatware, I discovered the XDA Developers community and flashed my first custom ROM. Got completely hooked.

2022

Going Deeper

Moved beyond flashing ROMs into kernel compilation, Magisk module development, and GSM tool experimentation. Started helping friends and neighbors with their devices.

2023

MasaDroid is Born

Word spread around Namanve and Mukono. Set up a proper workspace. Handled first 100 devices. Formalized services, pricing and the MasaDroid brand.

Now

Growing Every Day

500+ devices modded. Trusted by individuals, businesses and gaming communities across Uganda. Still learning, still improving, still loving every second of it.
